his Capstone project is not only the product of the last twelve weeks, but also marks the culmination of my four-year university journey. This Acknowledgements section extends beyond those who directly aided me in the execution of this project to encompass everyone who helped guide, support, and endure me throughout my engineering degree.

First, I would like to extend my gratitude to those who put up with me when I didn’t know what was cooking. A special thank you goes to the Radar Research Group for tolerating me as a first-year student and for showing immense patience when it took me two full weeks just to set up a Raspberry Pi.

I am deeply grateful to the lecturers who showed genuine passion for their fields and inspired me along the way. Thank you also to the Centre for High Performance Computing (CHPC) staff for providing the high-level computing resources and technical support that made handling our data possible.

To my supervisor, Robyn Verrinder: thank you for your endless generosity with your time. Thank you for trusting Emily and me with both the process and direction of our projects, as well as granting us free use of the BlueROV2—more affectionately known as ROVyn—and its accompanying laptop. Your mentorship and trust have been invaluable.

To my good friend Shaun Beutement, who taught me more about computers than any formal coursework ever did: thank you for sharing your knowledge, time, and sanity.

To Emily Slettevold: working alongside you has been one of the highlights of this capstone. This project did not just produce a final report and a dataset, but a lasting friendship.

To my new sister-in-law, Tristyn: your passion, yaps, and gossip made the last four years far more fun than rough, providing a much-needed breath of fresh air during the stress of engineering school.

I must also acknowledge the inanimate objects that made this report possible: the ARU coffee machine for keeping me caffeine-fueled, my wetsuit for keeping me warm during field work, and my laptop for surviving every test and render thrown its way.

Looking back, it is hard to put into words how much growth has occurred since the beginning. Reflecting on first-year Glen, sitting nervously in the orientation room among a room full of new faces, I could never have imagined the incredible community, challenges, and triumphs that lay ahead.

Finally, to my parents: thank you for your unconditional support, endless encouragement, and unwavering belief in me through every late night, tough exam, and milestone over the past four years. I could not have reached this finish line without you
